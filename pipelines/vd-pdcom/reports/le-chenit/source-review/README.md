# Le Chenit: PDCom currentness evidence from the 2026 PACom dossier

The official PACom ZIP is an adjacent source, not the original PDCom. The 109-page report identifies Council of State approval of the PDCom on **30 October 2000** (PDF page 20). Pages 105–106 explain how several historic development intentions were implemented, redirected or abandoned. The original PDCom map and its exact-version approval remain missing.

`findings.json` records seven grouped findings with page references, without inventing boundaries or counting them as sectors. In particular, almost occupied housing areas and proposed agricultural rezonings must not be treated as fresh development capacity. Currentness is what the June 2026 report states; later approval and actual construction status have not been independently verified.

`archive-manifest.json` records the official download URL, complete ZIP hash and member inventory. `reviewed-members.json` hashes the report, regulation and two PACom map sheets. `selected-page-text.json` preserves native report pages containing director-plan references. The full ZIP and PDFs are retained locally and excluded from Git.

The advertised public inquiry ran 10 June–9 July 2026. This review does not establish subsequent adoption or cantonal approval. No PDCom corpus, runtime geometry, parcel candidate, public delivery or completed-commune count changes.
