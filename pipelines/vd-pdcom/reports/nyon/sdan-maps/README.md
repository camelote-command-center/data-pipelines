# SDAN 2006 map and sector review

Ten original raster maps and 23 urban-concept legend symbols are extracted losslessly from the exact approved-report bytes. `inventory.json` records PDF/printed page, image xref, original dimensions, placement and content hash. `semantic-review.json` keeps four historical key-sector groups, their quantities and conditions separate. Map/legend review covers PDF12–13,20–23,30. Geographic validation remains pending.

The Eysins group describes about 1,000 additional residents and 400 jobs across its development sectors. Prangins describes about 3,000 residents and 2,000 jobs north of Route de l’Etraz. Nyon’s long-term 24ha reserve describes about 2,000–3,000 residents, conditional on first using densification opportunities toward 25,000 inhabitants. La Vuarpillière–Mondre–L’Asse describes about 1,400 jobs, explicitly dependent on enterprise type. These are 2006 strategic estimates, not current unused capacity; do not sum into present-day supply, allocate uniformly to polygons or overwrite newer communal plans.

The same visual hatch does not carry one universal category. Eysins’ local legend calls it a possible long-term housing option; Prangins calls it mixed housing/activities. Nyon/Vuarpillière detailed panels have no equivalent local legend on PDF22; category mapping remains unresolved. The overall PDF13 legend separately identifies potential inside existing building zones, densification, potential outside those zones, long-term outside-zone potential, and peripheral zones without densification. PDF13 requires detailed planning; PDF19 excludes an estimate of internal densification potential.

Thematic maps are raster, not vector: selected pages contain only two vector header/footer rules. The detailed panels range from 521 to 838 pixels per dimension. No georeferencing or polygon boundaries are supplied by this extraction. PDF30’s broad colored outlines define implementation workstreams, not development parcels; they are explicitly excluded from automatic sector extraction.

Reproduce with `python extract.py /path/to/exact-sdan.pdf` and verify with `python verify.py [--pdf /path/to/exact-sdan.pdf]`. SHA gates reject changed PDFs. Offline verification checks every asset hash/dimension, all 23 symbol labels, four distinct sector groups and review-manifest page references. With the exact PDF it also checks byte-for-byte inventory regeneration.
